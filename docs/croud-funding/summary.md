# Kickstarter Project Summary  
## Preservation and Restoration of Art by Holocaust Survivors

---

## 1. Purpose of the Project

This project aims to preserve and restore an original collection of paintings created by Jewish artists who survived the Holocaust and later lived and worked in Israel.

Due to extreme material scarcity during and after the war, many of these works were created on non-archival materials such as cardboard, industrial fabrics, and early canvas. Today, these materials are undergoing chemical and structural degradation that threatens the long-term survival of the artworks.

The purpose of the project is:
- to professionally restore these works to museum exhibition standards;
- to document the restoration process;
- to make the artworks and their stories publicly accessible;
- to enable planned museum exhibitions in Israel.

---

## 2. Why Kickstarter

Kickstarter is the appropriate platform for this project because:
- the project is cultural, educational, and non-commercial;
- it relies on community support rather than private ownership or investment;
- it allows transparent, all-or-nothing funding tied to a clearly defined restoration scope;
- it supports public-access outcomes (films, documentation, exhibitions).

The project does **not** offer financial returns, ownership rights, or commercial benefits to supporters. All rewards are symbolic acknowledgements of participation in cultural preservation.

---

## 3. Scope of the Collection

- Total artworks: **60 original paintings**
- Paintings requiring canvas conservation: **15**
- Paintings on cardboard / mixed materials: **45**

All 60 paintings require:
- frame repair;
- stabilization and archival mounting;
- documentation and photography;
- a short documentary video per artwork.

---

The project is currently in discussions with two Israeli museums:

### Netanya  
**Shem Olam – The Institute for Education, Documentation and Research on Faith and the Holocaust**  
https://www.shemolam.org.il/

### Haifa  
**Haifa Museum of Art**  
https://www.hma.org.il/eng

Both institutions have expressed **preliminary agreement** to exhibit the restored collection, subject to completion of conservation work and internal scheduling:

- **30 paintings** at Shem Olam (Netanya)  
- **30 paintings** at the Haifa Museum of Art  
- **60 works total**

Final exhibition dates and contracts are contingent on restoration completion and museum programming processes.
