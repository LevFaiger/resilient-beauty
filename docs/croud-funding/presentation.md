# Preservation and Restoration of Art by Holocaust Survivors

**Kickstarter Campaign**

---

## Purpose of the Project

Preserve and restore **60 original paintings** by Jewish artists who survived the Holocaust and later lived in Israel.

**The Challenge:**
- Works created on non-archival materials (cardboard, industrial fabrics)
- Chemical and structural degradation threatens survival

---

## Project Goals

- Professionally restore works to **museum exhibition standards**
- Document the restoration process
- Make artworks and stories **publicly accessible**
- Enable **museum exhibitions in Israel**

---

## Why Kickstarter

- Cultural, educational, **non-commercial** project
- Community support, not private investment
- Transparent, **all-or-nothing** funding
- Public-access outcomes (films, documentation, exhibitions)

**No financial returns or ownership rights** — only symbolic acknowledgements

---
