Heading: Urgent: Stop the Acid Decay
Technical Description:
Painted on non-archival cardboard during a time of extreme scarcity, this work is in a state of chemical crisis. The material is inherently acidic (low pH), causing the fibers to become brown, brittle, and prone to snapping.

The Risk: The support is "self-destructing" due to lignin-based acidity.

The Solution: Immediate chemical deacidification to neutralize the pH levels and archival mounting to prevent the board from crumbling.
