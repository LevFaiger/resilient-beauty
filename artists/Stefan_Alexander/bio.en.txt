🇨🇿 Czechoslovakia

Stefan Alexander (1910–1978)

Country of Origin: Czechoslovakia

Status: Holocaust Survivor

Biography

Stefan Alexander was born in 1910 in Czechoslovakia. He completed his studies at the Academy of Fine Arts in Prague and undertook further training in France, Italy, and Yugoslavia. He became a member of the Czech Academy of Arts.

War Years

During World War II, Alexander was sent to forced labor camps. He survived the Holocaust and resumed his artistic career in the years that followed.

Life and Career in Israel

From 1945 to 1949 he was active as a member of the Czech Academy of Arts in Prague. In 1949 he immigrated to Israel and eventually settled in Safed, where he became part of the city's artists' community and participated in its permanent exhibition. He also maintained ties with the Tel Aviv area.

Artistic Style and Subject Matter

Alexander worked as a painter and printmaker in a modernist vein. His oeuvre includes oils on canvas and masonite, watercolors and ink on paper, lithographs, and acrylic paintings. His subjects range from figurative and abstract compositions to landscapes and still lifes. His works are typically signed and dated.

Collections

Works by Stefan Alexander are documented in the Information Center for Israeli Art at the Israel Museum, Jerusalem. His paintings and prints appear in Israeli auction houses and private collections in Israel and abroad.
