Biography

Zita Weiss was born in Czechoslovakia in 1925. The Israel Museum lists her as an artist born in Czechia in 1925, an Auschwitz survivor, and an immigrant to Israel in 1950.

After arriving in Israel, Weiss became part of the Israeli artistic environment as a painter. According to the Israel Museum database, she lived in Haifa, and her name is included in the archive of Israeli art.
