🇵🇱 Poland / Lwów

Eliezer Greenberg (d. 1965)

Country of Origin: Poland, Lwów
today Ukraine

Status: Jewish Artist Who Survived the War; Helped Rescue Orphaned Jewish Children After the Holocaust

Biography

Eliezer Greenberg was born in Lwów, then part of Poland and today in Ukraine. His artistic talent was discovered at an early age by his uncle, who was an architect.

He studied at the School of Decorative Arts in Lwów and was later accepted to the Academy of Fine Arts in Kraków. Already as a young man, Greenberg received serious artistic training rooted in the cultural traditions of Central and Eastern Europe.

The War Years and the Soviet Union

With the outbreak of World War II, Greenberg was taken prisoner by the Russians. According to the biographical account, he was released after painting a portrait of Pyotr Tchaikovsky.

During the war years, he worked as a decorator in the Soviet Union and was accepted into the Russian Artists’ Association. This period became an important part of his professional life, while also reflecting the upheaval, displacement, and loss caused by the war.

In 1945, Greenberg discovered that only one sister had survived from his entire family. This tragedy became part of his personal story and connects his life to the destruction of European Jewry.

Helping Children Who Survived the Holocaust

After the war, Greenberg was involved in locating Jewish orphaned children who had survived the Holocaust and helping send them to Eretz Israel.

This activity gives his biography special significance: he was not only an artist who survived the war and the loss of his family, but also a person who took part in the rescue and renewal of Jewish life after the catastrophe.

Immigration to Israel

In 1949, Eliezer Greenberg immigrated to Israel aboard the ship Negba. After his arrival, he settled in Kiryat Motzkin, where he lived and worked until his death in 1965.

His life in Israel continued the journey of an artist whose biography connected Lwów, Kraków, the wartime Soviet Union, and the postwar rebuilding of Jewish life in the State of Israel.

Artistic Legacy

Eliezer Greenberg was an artist with serious academic training who worked in different artistic environments, from Lwów and Kraków to the Soviet Union and Israel.

His biography reflects the fate of a twentieth-century Jewish artist whose life was shaped by war, loss, and displacement, but who continued through creativity, assistance to surviving children, and participation in the cultural life of Israel.
