.🎨 Zvi Arman (1903–1993) - Israeli Painter, Holocaust Survivor, and ArchitectShort BiographyZvi Arman was born in Lviv (Lvov, then part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire, now Ukraine) in 1903. He studied art in Lviv and Kraków and was a member of the Jewish Artists Association.During World War II, he was imprisoned in the Lviv Ghetto but managed to escape to Caucasia. In 1947, he attempted to immigrate illegally to Palestine aboard the ship "Theodore Herzl" but was detained by the British and held in the internment camps in Cyprus.After immigrating to Israel, he initially worked as an architect. Concurrently, he joined the famous Artists' Colony in Safed (Tzfat), where he created art for many years.
