🇷🇴 Romania

Marcel Creta (Karta Marcel)

Country of Origin: Romania

Status: Holocaust-Era Refugee; Cyprus Detention Camp Detainee; Illegal Immigration (Ha'apala)

Biography

Marcel Creta — who signed his work Karta Marcel — was a Romanian Jewish painter active in the years surrounding the founding of the State of Israel. Little is known of his early life, but archival evidence places him among the tens of thousands of Jewish refugees who reached British-controlled Cyprus between 1945 and 1949 while attempting to immigrate to Palestine.

War Years, Ha'apala, and Cyprus

Between 1945 and 1949, some 59,000 Jewish immigrants passed through Cyprus, detained by the British authorities mainly at the Karaolos camp near Famagusta in an effort to limit immigration to Palestine. Detainees lived under harsh conditions in Nissan huts and tents, surrounded by barbed wire and kept under constant surveillance.

While held in Cyprus in 1948, Creta painted a series of oil works documenting daily life in the detention camps. As an artist-detainee, he sought to record the adversity of camp existence — the barbed wire, watchtowers, guards, searchlights, and communal tent life — and, in some scenes, the fragile hope suggested by morning light over the camp.

Life and Career in Israel

After his release from Cyprus, Creta continued to work as a painter in Israel. In early 1964 he held a solo exhibition of paintings in Tel Aviv at Beit Tzioni America (the American Zionist House). The exhibition is documented in ephemera preserved in the National Library of Israel and the Tel Aviv–Yafo Municipal Historical Archives.

Artistic Style and Subject Matter

Creta's known work is figurative and documentary in character. His Cyprus paintings depict the physical and emotional reality of detention: red barren earth enclosed by wire, watchtowers rising against the sky, white tents housing detainees, and figures resting before their shelters. Through color and composition he conveys both the misery of confinement and, at times, guarded anticipation of arrival in the Land of Israel.

Solo Exhibitions

1964 — Exhibition of paintings by Marcel Creta, Beit Tzioni America, Tel Aviv

Collections

Three oil paintings from Creta's 1948 Cyprus series — all titled Cyprus and signed Karta Marcel — are held in the collection of the Costas and Rita Severis Foundation (CVAR), Nicosia. Exhibition ephemera for his 1964 Tel Aviv show is preserved in the National Library of Israel, Jerusalem.
